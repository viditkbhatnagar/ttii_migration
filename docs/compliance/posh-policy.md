# POSH Policy — Prevention of Sexual Harassment

**Version:** 1.0 (draft) | **Effective Date:** {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}} | **Owner:** {{POSH_IC_PRESIDING_OFFICER}}, Presiding Officer of the Internal Committee
**Last reviewed:** {{LAST_REVIEW_DATE}} | **Next review:** {{NEXT_REVIEW_DATE}}

This Policy is issued under the **Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (Prevention, Prohibition and Redressal) Act, 2013** ("POSH Act") and the **Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (Prevention, Prohibition and Redressal) Rules, 2013** by {{LEGAL_ENTITY_NAME}} ("TTII"). It applies to every workplace of TTII — physical and virtual — including the TTII Platform itself.

## 1. Why this policy exists

Sexual harassment at the workplace is illegal, unethical, and corrosive. It is also the responsibility of every employer in India to prevent and redress it. TTII commits to a workplace — whether a physical office, a partner centre, a live class, a feed comment thread, or a one-on-one chat-support conversation — that is free of harassment.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

- **All TTII personnel**: directors, employees (full-time, part-time, probationary, intern, contract);
- **Instructors** and other independent contractors engaged by TTII;
- **Partner-centre staff** in their interactions with TTII personnel and TTII learners;
- **Visitors** to TTII offices and TTII events;
- **Learners** in their interactions with TTII personnel, instructors, and other learners on the Platform.

It applies whether the conduct occurs at the TTII office, at a partner centre, in a live class, in chat-support, in a feed comment, on email, on a phone call, in messages on personal devices, or anywhere else where a person is connected to TTII activity.

## 3. What constitutes sexual harassment

Per §2(n) of the POSH Act, sexual harassment includes any one or more of the following unwelcome acts or behaviour (whether directly or by implication):

1. Physical contact and advances;
2. A demand or request for sexual favours;
3. Sexually coloured remarks;
4. Showing pornography;
5. Any other unwelcome physical, verbal, or non-verbal conduct of a sexual nature.

The following circumstances, among other circumstances, may also amount to sexual harassment (§3(2)):

- Implied or explicit promise of preferential treatment in employment / programme delivery;
- Implied or explicit threat of detrimental treatment;
- Implied or explicit threat about present or future employment status / programme outcome;
- Interference with the person's work or academic performance, or creating an intimidating or offensive environment;
- Humiliating treatment likely to affect health or safety.

This policy interprets "workplace" broadly, in line with §2(o) of the POSH Act, to include all locations and tools where TTII work or learning happens — explicitly including the Platform's live classes, feed, chat-support, and direct messaging.

## 5. How to file a complaint

- A written complaint may be made by the **aggrieved woman** within **3 months** of the incident (or the last in a series). The IC may extend the period by up to 3 more months for good cause.
- If the woman is unable to file due to physical or mental incapacity, death, or other reason, a relative, friend, co-worker, or person with consent may file on her behalf.
- The complaint can be sent by email, post, or handed to any IC member or to {{POSH_IC_PRESIDING_OFFICER}} directly.
- The complaint should describe the incident, the date, the place, the persons involved, and any witnesses.

## 6. Confidentiality

The identity of the complainant, the respondent, witnesses, and the contents of the complaint and proceedings are kept **strictly confidential**, in line with §16 of the POSH Act. They will not appear in TTII's annual report. Information may be disclosed only to the persons who genuinely need to know, and to the District Officer / law enforcement where required by law.

Breach of confidentiality is itself a violation of this policy.

## 7. The process

1. **Receipt and preliminary review** — within 7 working days of receipt, the IC acknowledges and assigns the complaint.
2. **Conciliation** (only at the request of the complainant, before inquiry) — the IC may attempt settlement; no monetary settlement.
3. **Inquiry** — if conciliation is declined or fails, the IC conducts an inquiry within **90 days**. Both parties are heard; documents and witnesses examined; principles of natural justice are followed.
4. **Findings and report** — within **10 days** of conclusion, the IC submits its report to TTII (the "employer") with findings and recommendations. The report is also shared with the complainant and the respondent.
5. **Action by TTII** — TTII shall act on the IC's recommendations within **60 days**.
6. **Appeal** — either party may appeal to the appellate authority under the Industrial Employment (Standing Orders) Act / equivalent within 90 days of the recommendations (§18 POSH Act).

Where the complaint is against an instructor / centre staff / other contractor, TTII applies the same standards and may, in addition, terminate the engagement.

## 8. Interim relief

During the inquiry, the IC may recommend (and TTII shall give effect to):

- Transfer of either party to a different team or location;
- Leave to the complainant of up to 3 months;
- Restrictions on the respondent's contact with the complainant;
- Any other relief consistent with the POSH Act.

## 9. Sanctions on a finding of harassment

If the IC concludes harassment occurred, recommended actions include:

- Written apology to the complainant;
- Warning, censure, or reprimand;
- Withholding of promotion / pay raise / increment;
- Suspension or termination of employment / engagement;
- Counselling sessions;
- Community service;
- Where the conduct is criminal, referral to the police (FIR under §354A of the IPC, §509 IPC, IT Act §66E, POCSO Act 2012 if a child is involved, or other applicable provisions).

## 10. Malicious or false complaints

The POSH Act §14 makes a malicious or knowingly false complaint, or false evidence during inquiry, a punishable matter. However, **inability to substantiate a complaint or provide adequate proof is not the same as a malicious complaint** — only a finding of bad faith triggers §14.

## 11. No retaliation

Retaliation — direct or indirect — against a complainant, witness, or IC member is itself a serious violation of this policy. Allegations of retaliation are dealt with separately and may compound the original sanction.

## 12. Awareness and training

- This policy is delivered to every new joiner during induction.
- An annual refresher is conducted for all staff and instructors.
- IC members receive specialist training on POSH inquiry procedure.
- Awareness materials (posters, flyers) are displayed at the office and on the staff intranet.

## 13. Annual report

In line with §22 of the POSH Act, TTII files an annual report with the District Officer summarising:

- Number of complaints received during the year;
- Number disposed of;
- Number pending for more than 90 days;
- Number of workshops or awareness sessions conducted;
- Nature of action taken by the employer.

The report is filed by 30 April each year for the preceding calendar year.

## 14. Special application to the Platform

The Platform is a "workplace" for TTII personnel and includes a public-facing dimension where learners interact. This policy:

- Applies to harassment of any kind during a live class, in feed posts, in comments, in chat-support, or in direct messages;
- Empowers the [Grievance Officer](../legal/grievance-redressal-policy.md) to fast-track such complaints to the IC where the law applies;
- Provides for immediate platform sanctions (account suspension, content removal) pending IC inquiry, where there is *prima facie* indication of harassment.

## 15. Beyond the law's literal scope

The POSH Act 2013 protects "aggrieved women" specifically. TTII commits, **as a matter of policy**, to apply equivalent protections, processes, and outcomes to harassment complaints regardless of the gender of the complainant or respondent. Where the law does not formally extend, TTII's internal disciplinary process and the [Acceptable Use Policy](../legal/acceptable-use-policy.md) apply.
