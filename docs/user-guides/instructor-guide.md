# Instructor Guide

**Audience:** Instructors (role 3)
**Last reviewed:** {{LAST_REVIEW_DATE}}

This guide covers what you do as an instructor on TTII. New here? Start with [Getting Started](getting-started.md).

## Your work cycle

For every cohort you teach, the typical cycle is:

1. **Schedule a live class** (or review the schedule TTII set up for you);
2. **Host the class** — Microsoft Teams or Zoom, depending on the programme;
3. **Confirm attendance** (auto-synced for Teams; manual for Zoom and "manual link" sessions);
4. **Review and grade assignments**;
5. **Set, conduct, and evaluate examinations**;
6. **Respond to learners' questions** in chat / via the trainer's inbox;
7. **Submit periodic feedback** to TTII Operations.

## Live classes

### Joining as host

- Go to **Live Classes** in your portal.
- 5 minutes before start, the **Start Class** button appears. Click it; the platform creates the meeting (or opens the pre-created Teams / Zoom session) and admits you as host.
- For TTII Teams sessions, anonymous-trainer mode is supported — you do **not** need a Microsoft 365 license to host. Recording is automatic.
- Be on time; arrive 5 minutes early to test camera and microphone.

### During the class

- **Recording is on by default.** Confirm at the start of the session: "This session is being recorded for quality and instructional purposes." Tell learners.
- **Mute when not speaking.** Background noise is the most common quality complaint.
- **Engage**: ask questions, use chat, share visuals.
- **Watch attendance** — the platform shows joiners as they connect.

### After the class

- The recording is processed by Microsoft / Zoom and synced into TTII storage **5–45 minutes** later.
- Attendance for Teams syncs ~2 minutes after the session ends. For Zoom and manual sessions, mark attendance manually in **Live Classes → Attendance**.
- Add session notes for the cohort if you want.

## Assignments

### Setting an assignment

(If your role permits — some cohorts have assignments pre-set by TTII content team.)

- Title, description, due date, max marks, file-type allowed, max file size (default 50 MB).
- Optionally allow late submissions, and configure a late penalty.

### Reviewing submissions

- **Submissions list** — sortable by submission time, status, learner.
- Open a submission; download the file; provide feedback inline; assign a score.
- **Plagiarism / academic-integrity** — if you suspect a violation, flag the submission and notify the cohort owner. Do not score zero unilaterally without process — see the [Acceptable Use Policy](../legal/acceptable-use-policy.md) §2.4 on academic integrity.
- **Late submissions** — accept or reject per the assignment's settings.

## Examinations

### Setting an exam

- Pick from the question bank or create new questions;
- Configure: time limit, attempt limit, randomisation, proctoring rules;
- Schedule the exam window.

### Evaluation

- For objective questions, scoring is automatic;
- For subjective questions, evaluate via **Exam Evaluation**;
- Publish results when ready — until publication, learners see "Pending evaluation".

### Re-examinations

- If the cohort allows re-exams, learners register and pay (where applicable);
- Re-exams follow the same flow as the original.

## Communications

- **Cohort chat / trainer inbox** — learners can message you directly. Aim for a same-day or next-day response within {{SUPPORT_HOURS}}.
- **Live-class chat** — used during the session.
- **No personal channels** — please do not give learners your personal phone / WhatsApp. Channel everything through the platform so we have a record and can support you.

## Personal data discipline

You see learners' identity, contact, and academic data. You do **not** see their payment instruments or Aadhaar (unless your specific role requires it; default is no).

- Use this access only for instruction;
- Don't export learner data off-platform;
- Don't post learner names or images on social media without their consent;
- For accessibility / accommodation needs, treat the information confidentially. See the [Privacy Policy](../legal/privacy-policy.md) §3.5.

## Code of conduct in classrooms

- Treat every learner with respect, regardless of their background or pace;
- Do not single out learners in ways that could cause embarrassment;
- Apply the [Acceptable Use Policy](../legal/acceptable-use-policy.md) and the [POSH Policy](../compliance/posh-policy.md) at all times;
- If a learner reports a safety concern (their own or someone else's), escalate immediately to the [Grievance Officer](../legal/grievance-redressal-policy.md) and, where a minor's safety is at issue, also to the [Children's Privacy Notice](../legal/childrens-privacy-notice.md) escalation contact.

## Things to know

- **Recording during a live class** by your own screen-recorder is unnecessary — TTII records automatically. Don't keep personal copies of recordings.
- **You will not be charged** for any platform action by learners; if a learner says they "paid" you to pass an exam, this is a serious matter — report to TTII immediately.
- **Honoraria and payments to you** as instructor follow the trainer agreement. Questions about your remuneration go to {{ACCOUNTS_EMAIL}}.
