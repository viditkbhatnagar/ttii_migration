# Counsellor Quickstart

**Audience:** Counsellors (role 9) using `admin.teachersindia.in`
**Last reviewed:** {{LAST_REVIEW_DATE}}

You see a **scoped** version of the admin portal: Dashboard, Applications, Students, Counsellor Target, and Student Referrals. This guide covers your day-to-day.

## What you do

In short:

- Convert leads into applications;
- Convert applications into enrolled students;
- Hit your monthly target;
- Track referrals;
- Respond to learner queries that come into your inbox.

## Your portal sections

### Dashboard

A snapshot of:

- **Open applications you own**;
- **This month's progress vs. target** (calls made, applications created, applications converted, revenue);
- **Pending follow-ups**;
- **Recent referrals**.

### Applications

Your assigned applications. Filter by status, programme, intake, or due date for follow-up.

- **Open** an application to see the 3-tab detail (Application / Documents / Status & history);
- **Update status** (`pending`, `under review`, `quoted`, `payment received`, `converted`, `rejected`);
- **Log calls / notes** — these go into the application's history;
- **Send reminders** — payment, document upload;
- **Convert** — once payment is received and documents are verified.

### Students

The students that came from your applications. View profile, payment status, and progress. You generally cannot edit student data here — pass requests to Operations.

### Counsellor Target

Your assigned monthly / quarterly targets — calls, applications, conversions, revenue. Track progress; the dashboard updates in real time.

### Student Referrals

Learners who referred friends and the status / reward of each referral. Useful for nurturing word-of-mouth.

## Personal data discipline

Even with a smaller scope, you see real personal data. The same rules apply:

- Open a record **only when needed**;
- **No personal-channel communication.** Use the platform's logged channels — your call notes are part of the audit trail and protect both you and the applicant;
- **Aadhaar** — default-masked; reveal only when required and your action is logged;
- **No data exports off-platform.**

## Communication norms

- Be honest about programme price, duration, and certification status. Misrepresentation is a violation of the [Acceptable Use Policy](../legal/acceptable-use-policy.md) and may invalidate your conversions.
- Do not promise outcomes you cannot deliver (job placement, regulator equivalency).
- Do not pressure-sell. A learner who feels coerced is one chargeback or grievance away from a problem for both of us.
- Do not contact a lead after they have explicitly opted out.
