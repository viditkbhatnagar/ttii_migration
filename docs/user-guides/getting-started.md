# Getting Started with TTII

**Audience:** any new user — learner, centre administrator, instructor, counsellor, associate, TTII staff
**Last reviewed:** {{LAST_REVIEW_DATE}}

This is your first-day guide. It explains where to log in based on your role, how to set up your password, and what to do in your first hour.

## 1. The three portals

TTII has three websites. You will use **one** of them based on your role:

| You are a... | Go to | Roles |
|---|---|---|
| **Learner / student** | https://learn.teachersindia.in | Student |
| **Centre administrator or associate** | https://admissions.teachersindia.in | Centre, Associate |
| **TTII staff (admin, sub-admin, counsellor)** | https://admin.teachersindia.in | Admin, Sub-admin, Counsellor |
| **Instructor** | (instructor portal — link provided in your welcome email) | Instructor |

If you accidentally go to the wrong portal, the system will redirect you to the right one after login.

## 2. Activating your account

You will receive a welcome email with:

- The portal URL (one of the three above);
- Your username (usually your email or a TTII-issued enrolment ID);
- Either a temporary password or a "set your password" link.

### 2.1 First login with a temporary password

1. Go to the portal URL.
2. Enter your username and temporary password.
3. The system will ask you to set a new password before continuing.
4. Choose a strong password (at least 10 characters, mix of letters, numbers, and symbols, not reused from another site).

### 2.2 First login via a password-set link

1. Click the link in your welcome email.
2. Enter your new password twice.
3. The link is valid for **30 minutes**. If it expires, request a new one from the login page.

### 2.3 If you didn't receive a welcome email

- Check your spam / promotions folder;
- Verify with your centre administrator or programme coordinator that you were registered;
- Email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} with your full name and the programme you applied to.

## 3. Logging in

1. Open the portal URL in a modern browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Safari — current versions).
2. Enter username + password.
3. (For some flows) you may receive a 6-digit OTP by SMS or email. Enter it within 5 minutes.

If you fail to log in 5 times in 15 minutes you will be temporarily blocked. Wait, then either re-try or use the **Forgot Password** link.

## 4. Forgot your password?

1. On the login screen, click **Forgot Password**.
2. Enter the email associated with your account.
3. You will receive an email with a reset link.
4. Click the link **within 30 minutes** and set a new password.
5. The reset link is single-use and is invalidated when you change your password.

If the email doesn't arrive: check spam, then email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}.

## 5. First-hour checklist

Once you can log in, take 15 minutes to:

| Task | Where | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Update your profile | Your portal's **Profile** or **Settings** page | Make sure your name, email, and phone are correct so you receive notifications |
| Upload a profile photo (optional) | Profile page | Helps trainers recognise you in live classes |
| Verify your phone number (if prompted) | Profile page | Required for OTP-based recovery |
| Read the relevant manual | [admin](admin-user-manual.md) / [student](student-user-manual.md) / [centre](centre-admissions-manual.md) / [instructor](instructor-guide.md) / [counsellor](counsellor-quickstart.md) | Saves you time later |
| Skim the [FAQs](faqs.md) | This pack | Most first-day questions are answered here |
| Skim the [Privacy Policy](../legal/privacy-policy.md) | This pack | Know what data we collect and your rights |

## 6. Your data and your rights

When you sign up to TTII you give us certain personal information. Here is the short version of how we treat it:

- We use your data to deliver the programme you signed up for and to comply with the law.
- We don't sell your data. We don't run advertising trackers on you.
- You can ask for a copy of your data, correct it, or delete it — see the [DPDP Rights Request Form](../legal/dpdp-rights-request-form.md).
- For complaints, contact our Grievance Officer ({{GRIEVANCE_OFFICER_EMAIL}}).

The detailed version is in the [Privacy Policy](../legal/privacy-policy.md).

## 7. Stay safe online

- Never share your password — TTII staff will never ask for it.
- If an email asks you to click a link and log in, **navigate to the portal yourself** instead of clicking. Phishing is real.
- Lock your screen when you step away.
- Use a private browser window if you're using a shared computer.
- Sign out when you're done.
