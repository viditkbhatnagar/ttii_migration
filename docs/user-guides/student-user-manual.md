# Student User Manual

**Audience:** Learners using `learn.teachersindia.in` (role 2)
**Last reviewed:** {{LAST_REVIEW_DATE}}

This manual walks through every page on the Learner Portal. New to TTII? Start with [Getting Started](getting-started.md), then come back here.

## Layout

After login you land on the **Dashboard**. The left sidebar gives you the seven sections:

1. **Dashboard**
2. **Learning** (your courses)
3. **Assessments** (exams, assignments)
4. **Payments**
5. **Notifications**
6. **Profile**
7. **Support**

---

## 1. Dashboard

A snapshot of the most relevant things right now:

- **Upcoming live classes** — date, time, course, "Join" button when the session is live;
- **Pending assignments** — anything due in the next 7 days;
- **Recent grades** — the latest exam or assignment you got back;
- **Unread notifications** — quick count;
- **Outstanding fees** — if any;
- **Quick links** — your enrolled courses, your payments page, your support inbox.

If nothing is happening on a given day, the Dashboard is calm. That's by design.

---

## 2. Learning

Your enrolled courses appear here. Click a course to open it.

### Inside a course

- **Syllabus / lessons** — the ordered list of lessons. Each lesson has a status: not-started, in-progress, completed.
- **Lesson types**:
  - **Video** — plays in-browser. Use the speed control if 1× is too slow / too fast. Captions when available.
  - **Document** (PDF) — opens in a viewer. Download where allowed.
  - **Audio** — plays in-browser.
  - **Article** — long-form text inside the platform.
  - **Quiz** — auto-graded mini-test inside a lesson.
- **Live classes for the course** — dates, durations, join links once the trainer starts.
- **Recordings** — past sessions, available shortly after the session ends. Play in-browser; download is disabled by design.
- **Resources** — additional reading material your trainer has shared.

### Marking progress

Lesson progress is tracked automatically. If you watch ≥90% of a video, the lesson is marked complete. For documents and articles, scrolling to the end marks complete. For quizzes, a passing score marks the lesson complete.

### Live classes

- The "Join" button appears 5 minutes before the scheduled start.
- Clicking Join opens the meeting in Microsoft Teams or Zoom. You may need to allow camera / microphone access.
- Be ready: have a stable connection, headphones, and a quiet space if you're going to speak.
- Be on time — many trainers lock the room after the start.
- Recording is on by default. By joining, you accept that the session is recorded — see the [Privacy Policy](../legal/privacy-policy.md).

## 3. Assessments

Two things live here: exams and assignments.

### Exams

The Exams tab lists exams scheduled for your enrolled courses. For each exam you see: course, date, duration, status.

- **Before the window** — you can read the exam description and any preparation material.
- **During the window** — click **Start** to begin. There is a per-exam time limit. **Don't refresh the page** mid-exam unless you have to.
- **After the window** — your attempt is saved. Results appear once the trainer publishes them.

**Re-examination** — if a re-exam is offered, register from the Re-Exam tab. There may be a re-exam fee.

### Assignments

For each course you can see assignments with a due date and status: not submitted / submitted / graded.

- **Submit** uploads a file (PDF / DOC / image) up to 50 MB.
- **Late submission** is allowed only if the trainer permits it; otherwise the late attempt is rejected.
- **Feedback** — after grading, the trainer's feedback and your score appear inline.
- **Re-submission** — only if the trainer asks for it.

## 4. Payments

Everything money-related, in one place.

- **Outstanding balance** — what you owe and the due date.
- **Pay now** — opens Razorpay; pay by card, UPI, net-banking, or wallet. Once successful, the balance updates within a few minutes.
- **Installments** — if your programme is on a payment plan, the schedule and status of each instalment.
- **Payment history** — every past payment with date, mode, amount, status, and a downloadable invoice.
- **Coupons** — apply a coupon code at checkout if you have one.

If a payment fails, you can retry. If your bank says the payment went through but TTII still shows it as outstanding, give it 30 minutes — Razorpay's webhook usually settles within a few minutes. If it doesn't, email {{ACCOUNTS_EMAIL}} with the Razorpay transaction ID.

For refunds and cancellations, see the [Refund & Cancellation Policy](../legal/refund-cancellation-policy.md).

---

## 5. Notifications

A list of platform-generated messages: course updates, exam announcements, fee reminders, certificate issuance, circulars from TTII admins.

- **Unread** appears at the top in bold; click to open and mark read.
- **Filter** by category (general, exam, payment, certificate, circular).
- **Push notifications** — if you've allowed them on this device, important alerts also pop on your phone.

---

## 6. Profile

Your personal information.

- **Edit name, email, phone, address.** Some fields are read-only because they affect academic records — contact your centre or {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} to change them.
- **Change password** — requires your current password.
- **Profile photo** — upload an image up to 50 MB; it appears on your dashboard, on certificates if the programme uses a photo, and to your trainer.
- **Languages spoken** — used for accommodation and to match you with appropriate trainers.
- **Accessibility needs** — used confidentially to provide reasonable accommodations.
- **Marketing preferences** — opt in / out of non-essential communications.
- **Sign out everywhere** (when available) — invalidates your sessions on all devices.

### Your data rights

From this page you can also:

- **Download your data** (where the self-serve endpoint is live; otherwise email {{DPO_EMAIL}});
- **Request deletion** — you'll be guided through the process.

See the [Privacy Policy](../legal/privacy-policy.md) and the [DPDP Rights Request Form](../legal/dpdp-rights-request-form.md).

## 7. Support

A built-in chat with TTII support staff.

- **Start a new conversation** — pick a category (technical / billing / academic / other) and type your question.
- **Attach a screenshot** if it helps.
- **Wait time** — typical first response within {{SUPPORT_HOURS}}.
- **History** — previous conversations remain available for your reference.
- **Escalation** — if you're not satisfied, you can escalate to the Grievance Officer ({{GRIEVANCE_OFFICER_EMAIL}}). See the [Grievance Redressal Policy](../legal/grievance-redressal-policy.md).

## Common tasks

| Task | Where |
|---|---|
| Find my next live class | Dashboard → Upcoming live classes |
| Watch a missed live class | Course → Recordings |
| Pay an outstanding fee | Payments → Pay now |
| Submit an assignment | Course → Assignments → Submit |
| Get my certificate | Issued automatically when completion criteria are met; download from the course page or the Certificates section once available |
| See my exam scores | Assessments → Exams (after the trainer publishes results) |
| Update my phone number | Profile → Edit |
| Reset my password | Profile → Change password (or Forgot Password from the login page) |
| Get help | Support → New conversation |

---

## Things to know

- **Sessions expire after about an hour of inactivity.** You'll have to log in again. This is for your security.
- **Your password is private.** TTII staff will never ask for it.
- **Be careful in feed and chat.** Treat other learners and trainers respectfully — see the [Acceptable Use Policy](../legal/acceptable-use-policy.md).
- **Recording your screen during a live class** is a privacy violation and a breach of the AUP.
- **If something looks wrong**, report it via Support or via {{GRIEVANCE_OFFICER_EMAIL}}.
