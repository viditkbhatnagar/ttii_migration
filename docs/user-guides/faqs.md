## A. Account & login

**A.1. Which portal do I use?**
- Learner → https://learn.teachersindia.in
- Centre / Associate → https://admissions.teachersindia.in
- Staff → https://admin.teachersindia.in
- Instructor → link in your welcome email

If you go to the wrong one, the system will redirect you after login.

**A.2. I didn't get my welcome email.**
Check spam, then email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} with your full name and the programme / centre you applied to.

**A.3. I forgot my password.**
Click **Forgot Password** on the login screen. You'll get an email with a reset link valid for 30 minutes.

**A.4. The reset link doesn't work / has expired.**
Request a new one. If you keep hitting expiry, your inbox may be flagging the email as spam — check there. If the issue persists, contact {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}.

**A.5. The system says "too many attempts, try later".**
You hit the rate limit (5 logins / 5 password resets / 5 OTP requests per 15 minutes). Wait 15 minutes, then try again, or use Forgot Password if you're stuck.

**A.6. Can I have my email / phone changed?**
Some fields are self-serve from your Profile page. Ones tied to academic records (enrolment ID, sometimes name) need to go through your centre or {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}.

**A.7. How long do sessions last?**
About 1 hour from your last activity. After that, you log in again.

**A.8. Will I be told if my account was logged in from a new place?**
The platform logs every authentication; staff review anomalies. Per-user "new-device" notifications are on the roadmap.

## B. Privacy & data

**B.1. What data do you collect?**
See the [Privacy Policy](../legal/privacy-policy.md) §3 for the complete list.

**B.2. Do you sell my data?**
No.

**B.3. Do you advertise to me?**
No. We do not run advertising or behavioural-tracking pixels on the Platform.

**B.4. Can I get a copy of my data?**
Yes. Submit the [DPDP Rights Request Form](../legal/dpdp-rights-request-form.md) or email {{DPO_EMAIL}}.

**B.5. Can I delete my account?**
Yes. Same form. Note that some data (tax invoices for the past 7 years, certificates) may need to be retained by law — we'll explain what stays and why.

**B.6. Why did you ask for my Aadhaar?**
Only some programmes require Aadhaar — for KYC linked to a partner / scheme. You can ask for an alternative ID; if the programme genuinely requires Aadhaar and you decline, we may not be able to enrol you. See the [Aadhaar Handling Policy](../compliance/aadhaar-handling-policy.md).

**B.7. Where is my data stored?**
On infrastructure in India (Bangalore) for the database, Singapore for object storage. Some communications and conferencing services are international (USA / EU). Full list in the [Subprocessor List](../compliance/subprocessor-list.md).

**B.8. I'm a parent. How do I see my child's data?**
Submit the [DPDP Rights Request Form](../legal/dpdp-rights-request-form.md) with proof of guardianship. Also see the [Children's Privacy Notice](../legal/childrens-privacy-notice.md).

## C. Courses & live classes

**C.1. The live-class link doesn't work.**
- Check that the session has started (the "Join" button appears 5 minutes before).
- Make sure you've allowed pop-ups in your browser.
- Some networks (school / corporate) block Microsoft Teams / Zoom; try a different network or your mobile hotspot.
- See [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md) → "Can't join live class".

**C.2. I missed a live class. Can I watch the recording?**
Yes — recordings are available from the course page within ~5–45 minutes of the session ending (Microsoft processes the recording first).

**C.3. Why can't I download the recording?**
By design — recordings are streamed via short-lived signed URLs. This protects the trainer, other learners, and copyright.

**C.4. The video is buffering / slow.**
- Lower the video quality;
- Check your network speed;
- Try a different browser;
- If the problem only happens for one specific video, message support — the source file may need re-upload.

**C.5. The course content seems wrong / outdated.**
Email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} with the course name, lesson, and what you saw. The content team appreciates corrections.

## D. Exams & certificates

**D.1. When will I get my exam result?**
After the trainer publishes results. For automatic-grade exams, that's usually within 24 hours; for subjective exams, it can take up to 2 weeks.

**D.2. I didn't pass. Can I retake?**
Only if the programme allows re-examinations. Check the **Re-Exam** tab; there may be a fee.

**D.3. When will I get my certificate?**
When you meet the completion policy: required attendance, exam scores, assignments, and any manual approval. The Certificate appears on your dashboard / certificates page automatically. If a "manual approval" is required, an admin reviews before issuance.

**D.4. The certificate has my name spelt wrong.**
- For an unissued certificate: update your Profile, then ask Support to refresh;
- For an issued certificate: write to {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} with proof of correct spelling. There may be a small reissue fee.

**D.5. Is the TTII certificate recognised by [employer / regulator]?**
Each programme states recognition explicitly. Read the programme description before enrolling. We do not promise recognition that isn't expressly stated. See the [Disclaimer](../legal/disclaimer.md) §3.

## E. Payments

**E.1. I paid but my dues still show outstanding.**
Wait 30 minutes — Razorpay's webhook usually updates within minutes but can take longer. If it doesn't update, email {{ACCOUNTS_EMAIL}} with the Razorpay transaction ID.

**E.2. Can I get a refund?**
See the [Refund & Cancellation Policy](../legal/refund-cancellation-policy.md) for the windows and process.

**E.3. Can I pay in instalments?**
If your programme offers an instalment plan, you'll see it at checkout. Otherwise, payment is upfront.

**E.4. I got a duplicate charge.**
Email {{ACCOUNTS_EMAIL}} with both transaction IDs; we'll reconcile and refund the duplicate.

**E.5. Can I get a tax invoice with my GSTIN?**
Yes. Update your billing details in Profile before paying so the GSTIN appears on the invoice. For corrections after the invoice is issued, contact {{ACCOUNTS_EMAIL}}.

## F. Centres

**F.1. Why can't I see another centre's data?**
By design. Each centre's view is scoped to its own data per the [Access Control Policy](../security/access-control-policy.md).

**F.2. How do I top up my wallet?**
Wallet → Top-up. Online via Razorpay or by NEFT / cheque to TTII (offline top-ups are added by Accounts after verification).

**F.3. A learner's parent is asking for a copy of their child's data.**
Direct them to the [DPDP Rights Request Form](../legal/dpdp-rights-request-form.md). Don't fulfil from your side — TTII handles the request centrally with identity verification.

## H. The AI Mentor

**H.1. What is the AI Mentor?**
An optional feature where you can ask a question and get an AI-generated answer. Powered by OpenAI.

**H.2. Is my prompt private?**
Your prompt is sent to OpenAI under our [DPA](../compliance/data-processing-agreement-template.md). OpenAI does not use it to train. Don't paste sensitive data (Aadhaar, payment, names of other people) into the AI Mentor.

**H.3. Can I trust the answers?**
Treat AI answers as a starting point. Always verify against your trainer or course material. See the [Disclaimer](../legal/disclaimer.md) §5.
