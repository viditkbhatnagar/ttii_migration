# Acceptable Use Policy

**Version:** 1.0 (draft) | **Effective Date:** {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}} | **Owner:** {{GRIEVANCE_OFFICER_NAME}}
**Last reviewed:** {{LAST_REVIEW_DATE}} | **Next review:** {{NEXT_REVIEW_DATE}}

This Acceptable Use Policy ("**AUP**") sets the rules for what you may and may not do on the TTII Platform. It applies to all users — learners, applicants, centres, associates, instructors, counsellors, and TTII staff. It is referenced from, and subject to, the [Terms of Service](terms-of-service.md). Violations may result in content removal, account suspension or termination, forfeiture of certificates, refund denial, and where applicable, legal action.

## 1. The basics

Be honest, civil, and respectful. The Platform is a learning environment used by adults and, in some programmes, minors. Treat every other user as you would expect to be treated.

## 2. What you must not do

You must not, on any part of the Platform — including assignment uploads, feed posts, comments, chat-support messages, live-class chat, profile photos, or any other surface where you can submit content:

### 2.1 Unlawful content

- Post or share content that is unlawful under Indian law, including obscene, pornographic, or sexually explicit material;
- Post **any** content involving minors that is sexually suggestive or exploitative — such material is reported to law enforcement immediately under the **POCSO Act 2012**;
- Post content that incites violence, terrorism, or hatred against any person or group on the basis of religion, race, caste, gender, sexual orientation, place of birth, language, or any other protected ground;
- Post content that threatens public order, sovereignty, or the integrity of India;
- Post content that defames or knowingly publishes false information about any person.

### 2.2 Harassment and abuse

- Bully, harass, intimidate, threaten, or impersonate another user;
- Engage in sexual harassment in any form. Sexual harassment as defined in the **Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (Prevention, Prohibition and Redressal) Act, 2013** ("POSH Act") is a zero-tolerance violation. See the [POSH Policy](../compliance/posh-policy.md) for the complaint mechanism;
- Stalk, dox, or repeatedly contact someone who has asked to be left alone;
- Engage in coordinated harassment ("brigading") of another user.

### 2.3 Privacy violations

- Share another person's personal data — phone, address, ID, photograph — without their explicit consent;
- Record a live class with personal recording tools when the trainer has not authorised it (the platform's own recording is governed by the [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md));
- Share screen recordings or screenshots of other learners' private profiles, identifiers, or messages.

### 2.4 Academic integrity

- Impersonate another candidate in an examination or assignment, or arrange for another person to do so;
- Use unauthorised aids in an examination (notes, textbooks, electronic devices, AI tools where forbidden by the assignment, communication with other candidates);
- Submit work that is not your own as your own — including plagiarised text, AI-generated text presented as original where the assignment forbids it, and direct copies of another learner's submission;
- Share examination questions, answer keys, or assignment solutions with other learners during the live window;
- Falsify attendance, identity documents, qualifications, or supporting evidence in an application.

Academic-integrity violations are handled by an internal committee with rights of response. Sanctions may include marking the assignment / exam zero, repeating the programme at your cost, forfeiting the certificate, and termination of the account.

### 2.5 Intellectual property

- Reproduce, redistribute, modify, post, or commercially exploit course videos, recorded live classes, examinations, or any other Content;
- Strip or obscure copyright or watermark notices on any Content;
- Use TTII logos, trade marks, or branding except as expressly authorised in writing;
- Use the Platform's Content to train any machine-learning model.

### 2.6 Spam and unsolicited communications

- Send unsolicited bulk messages or commercial pitches to other learners;
- Set up multiple accounts, or use bots / scripts, to send messages or post comments at scale;
- Post off-topic or repetitive material designed to drown out genuine discussion.

### 2.7 Security and integrity

- Probe, scan, or test the vulnerability of any system on the Platform without prior written consent (see [Vulnerability Disclosure Policy](../security/vulnerability-disclosure-policy.md));
- Bypass or attempt to bypass authentication, rate limits, paywalls, or access controls;
- Upload, transmit, or link to malware, viruses, worms, ransomware, or any other harmful code;
- Scrape, mass-download, or otherwise collect data from the Platform other than data you create or are explicitly granted access to;
- Reverse-engineer, decompile, or disassemble Platform software except as expressly permitted by law;
- Use automated tools to register accounts, take examinations, or post content;
- Attempt to access another user's account.

### 2.8 Misuse of payment and identity

- Use a stolen, fraudulent, or otherwise unauthorised payment instrument;
- Knowingly initiate a chargeback for a charge that is not in dispute;
- Submit forged documents, photographs, or signatures.

### 2.9 Misrepresentation

- Pretend to be a TTII employee, instructor, or representative when you are not;
- Misrepresent your affiliation with a centre, school, or partner;
- Misrepresent the authority you have to enrol or transact on behalf of another person.

## 3. Special rules for live classes

- Mute your microphone unless invited to speak;
- Keep your video on if the trainer requests it for attendance verification, except where you have a legitimate reason (privacy, bandwidth, accessibility);
- Do not use offensive virtual backgrounds or display names;
- Do not invite or admit unauthorised participants. The trainer may admit only enrolled learners;
- Do not record the session except via the Platform's own recording controls. Recording the session yourself is a privacy violation and may be a breach of the IT Act.

## 4. Special rules for the feed and comments

- Stay on topic;
- Critique ideas, not people;
- Disclose your role if you are a TTII employee or instructor commenting in an official capacity;
- Do not promote external products or services without prior written consent.

## 5. Special rules for staff

TTII staff (Admin, Sub-admin, Counsellor, Instructor) have privileged access to learners' personal data. Staff additionally must:

- Access learners' data only on a strict need-to-know basis to perform their job;
- Not export, copy, or share learner data outside TTII systems unless an approved process requires it;
- Use TTII-issued accounts and devices for TTII work; not store learner data on personal devices;
- Lock screens when stepping away from a workstation;
- Report suspected security incidents to {{SECURITY_EMAIL}} immediately;
- Comply with the [Information Security Policy](../security/information-security-policy.md), [Access Control Policy](../security/access-control-policy.md), and [Acceptable Asset Use Policy](../security/acceptable-asset-use-policy.md).

## 6. Reporting violations

If you see content or behaviour that violates this AUP:

- Use the in-product **Report** option where available;
- Email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} for general reports;
- Email {{GRIEVANCE_OFFICER_EMAIL}} to file a formal grievance under the [Grievance Redressal Policy](grievance-redressal-policy.md);
- Email {{POSH_IC_EMAIL}} for sexual-harassment complaints — handled in confidence by the Internal Committee;
- Email {{SECURITY_EMAIL}} to report suspected security incidents.

## 7. How we respond

We may, without notice and at our discretion:

- Remove or hide offending content;
- Issue a warning;
- Suspend the account temporarily or permanently;
- Forfeit certificates already issued where they were procured by violation of this AUP;
- Withhold or reverse refunds where the violation forfeits the right to a refund under the [Refund & Cancellation Policy](refund-cancellation-policy.md);
- Report the matter to law enforcement;
- Cooperate with regulators and judicial proceedings.

We document each enforcement action and apply principles of natural justice. The user complained against will normally be given an opportunity to respond before a final decision, unless the violation is so severe (for example child safety, imminent threat) that immediate action is required.

## 8. Appeals

If you believe enforcement action against your account was wrong, write to {{GRIEVANCE_OFFICER_EMAIL}} within 30 days. We will review and respond per the [Grievance Redressal Policy](grievance-redressal-policy.md).

## 9. Updates

We may update this AUP from time to time. The "**Effective Date**" line at the top reflects the current version. Material changes will be notified by in-product banner.
