This policy is published under **Rule 3(11) of the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021** and **Rule 5(9) of the SPDI Rules, 2011**, and supplements the rights granted to data principals under the **Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023**. It explains how {{LEGAL_ENTITY_NAME}} handles complaints relating to the TTII Platform.

## 1. Scope

You may use this policy to raise a grievance about:

- Content on the Platform you believe is unlawful, defamatory, infringing, or otherwise violates the [Acceptable Use Policy](acceptable-use-policy.md);
- Privacy or personal-data concerns (incorrect data, unauthorised processing, refusal of a rights request);
- Service issues (course delivery, certificates, examinations, payments);
- Conduct by another user (peer, instructor, centre, or staff), including harassment;
- Suspected security incidents (please also see our [Vulnerability Disclosure Policy](../security/vulnerability-disclosure-policy.md));
- Any other complaint where you believe TTII has not met its obligations.

## 3. How to raise a grievance

Send an email or letter to the Grievance Officer with the following information. Incomplete grievances delay our response.

1. **Your name** and contact details (the email and phone associated with your account);
2. **The portal you use** (learn / admissions / admin);
3. **The nature of the grievance**, with specifics (dates, what you saw, what you expected);
4. **Evidence** where available — screenshots, links, payment receipts, order IDs, names of other users involved;
5. **The remedy you seek** — for example, removal of content, correction of personal data, refund, apology, sanction against another user;
6. **Your willingness to be contacted** by phone if we need to clarify something;
7. **Your signature** (typed name is acceptable in email).

We will acknowledge the grievance via email and / or in-product notification.

## 5. What we will do

For each grievance we will:

- Assign a **case reference number** and a single **assigned investigator**;
- **Investigate** the facts, including reviewing system logs, content, account history, and where appropriate consulting the user complained against;
- **Communicate** with you in writing, in plain language, about findings;
- **Apply remedies** where warranted: removing or labelling content, correcting personal data, refunding fees, suspending or terminating accounts, escalating to law enforcement;
- **Document** the investigation and outcome for our records and regulatory reporting;
- **Improve** — recurring complaint patterns are reviewed quarterly to drive systemic fixes.

## 6. Grievances about other users

If your grievance concerns another user (peer, instructor, centre staff, employee), we follow the principles of natural justice:

- We will normally not disclose your identity to the user complained against unless necessary to investigate or you specifically consent;
- We will give the user complained against a fair opportunity to respond;
- We will not pre-judge the matter; sanctions, where applied, follow a documented finding.

For grievances of sexual harassment, the [POSH Policy](../compliance/posh-policy.md) applies. The Internal Committee constituted under the POSH Act handles those matters in confidence.

## 7. Escalation if you are not satisfied

If you are not satisfied with the Grievance Officer's response, you may:

1. Write to the **Nodal Officer** at {{NODAL_OFFICER_EMAIL}} for a senior review.
2. For privacy and personal-data matters, write to the **Data Protection Officer** at {{DPO_EMAIL}}.
3. **Escalate externally**:
   - **Data Protection Board of India** — once notified by the Government, you may approach the Board under §13 of the DPDP Act for unresolved data-protection complaints.
   - **Consumer Commissions** (District / State / National) — for consumer-protection matters under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019.
   - **Cyber Crime Reporting** — for offences under the IT Act, file a complaint at https://cybercrime.gov.in or with the local police.
   - **Civil and criminal courts** with appropriate jurisdiction.
4. For matters specifically under the SPDI Rules, you may also approach the Adjudicating Officer notified under §46 of the IT Act.

## 8. Children and persons unable to act for themselves

A grievance may be filed on behalf of a minor or a person unable to act for themselves by their parent, legal guardian, or duly appointed representative. We may ask for evidence of the relationship before proceeding.

## 9. Privacy of grievance records

Information you share with the Grievance Officer is treated as confidential and used only for the purposes of investigating and resolving the grievance, complying with legal obligations, and improving the Platform. Records are retained per the [Data Retention & Deletion Policy](../security/data-retention-deletion-policy.md).

## 10. No retaliation

You will not face retaliation, account closure, or denial of service for filing a good-faith grievance. Filing a knowingly false grievance, however, may itself be a ground for action under the [Terms of Service](terms-of-service.md).

## 11. Annual transparency

In line with IT Rules 2021 expectations of large intermediaries, we publish an annual summary of:

- The number of grievances received, by category;
- The number resolved, the number pending, and the median time to resolution;
- The number and type of take-downs.

The first transparency report will cover the period from {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}} to one year thereafter and will be published at https://teachersindia.in.
