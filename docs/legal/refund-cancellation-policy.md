# Refund & Cancellation Policy

**Version:** 1.0 (draft) | **Effective Date:** {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}} | **Owner:** {{ACCOUNTS_EMAIL}}
**Last reviewed:** {{LAST_REVIEW_DATE}} | **Next review:** {{NEXT_REVIEW_DATE}}

This policy describes when {{LEGAL_ENTITY_NAME}} ("TTII") refunds course fees, how we process refunds, and when refunds are not available. It is part of and read together with our [Terms of Service](terms-of-service.md). It complies with the **Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020**, and the GST law on credit notes.

## 1. Scope

This policy applies to:

- All paid programmes purchased through `learn.teachersindia.in`;
- Centre-driven enrolments processed via `admissions.teachersindia.in` (subject to additional terms in the centre's contract);
- Application fees, examination fees, re-examination fees, and any other paid item on the Platform.

It does **not** apply to:

- Goods or services purchased outside the Platform;
- Bundled programmes purchased through a co-branded partner (refer to the partner's policy in addition to this one).

## 2. Cooling-off period

You may cancel and request a full refund within **{{REFUND_WINDOW_DAYS}} calendar days** of payment, **provided that you have not consumed Content beyond the trial portion** (defined below).

Trial portion means: the first lesson of any course, course preview videos, the syllabus / curriculum description, or any sample assignment marked as preview. Watching, reading, or downloading any Content beyond the trial portion ends the cooling-off period.

## 3. Withdrawal after course start

If you wish to withdraw after the cooling-off period has ended:

| When you withdraw | Refund |
|---|---|
| Within the first {{COURSE_WITHDRAWAL_DAYS}} days of the course start date and you have completed less than 25% of Content | 50% of fees paid for the affected programme, less processing charges |
| After {{COURSE_WITHDRAWAL_DAYS}} days, **or** after completing 25% or more of Content, whichever is earlier | No refund — you may continue to access Content until the programme ends |

"Completion of Content" is measured by lesson watch / read tracking on the Platform.

## 4. Cancellations by us

We may cancel a programme (in part or in full) where there is a force-majeure event, where minimum enrolments are not met, where a key instructor is unavailable, or for any other reason that materially prevents delivery. In that case:

- If the programme has not started, we will refund the full Course Fee;
- If the programme has started, we will offer either a pro-rata refund for the un-delivered portion or a transfer to an equivalent programme, at your election.

## 5. Refunds for examinations and re-examinations

| Item | Refund treatment |
|---|---|
| Examination fee, before the exam window opens | Refundable, less processing charges |
| Examination fee, after the exam window opens | Non-refundable; you may reschedule once if eligible |
| Examination fee, after you have appeared | Non-refundable |
| Re-examination fee | Non-refundable once you have registered |

A failure to pass an examination is **not** grounds for refund.

## 6. Refunds for application and enrolment fees

Application fees (paid at the time of submitting an application) are refundable only if we reject your application before processing it. Once an application is accepted and an enrolment offer is issued, the application fee becomes non-refundable; it is treated as part of the Course Fee paid towards the programme.

## 7. When refunds are not available

We do not issue a refund where:

- You have breached the [Terms of Service](terms-of-service.md), the [Acceptable Use Policy](acceptable-use-policy.md), or the academic-integrity rules;
- Your account has been suspended or terminated for cause;
- The programme has been substantially delivered to you (75% or more of Content consumed);
- The refund is requested outside the windows above and no force-majeure event applies;
- Payment was made with a coupon, credit, scholarship, or sponsor-paid arrangement (the issuer's terms apply);
- The fee paid is for a third-party service we resell on a no-refund basis (where this is disclosed at the point of purchase).

## 8. How to request a refund

Email {{ACCOUNTS_EMAIL}} from your registered email address with:

- Your full name and registered phone number;
- The programme or item you are seeking a refund for;
- The order / payment reference (Razorpay order ID where available, or a copy of the payment receipt);
- The reason for the refund.

We acknowledge refund requests within **{{GRIEVANCE_ACK_HOURS}} hours** and decide within **7 working days** of receiving complete information.

## 9. How refunds are processed

- **Online payments (Razorpay)** — refunded to the original payment instrument. Razorpay typically credits the refund within 5–10 working days, but the actual time depends on your bank or card network.
- **Offline payments (cheque / DD / NEFT / cash)** — refunded by NEFT / IMPS to a bank account in your name. You must provide accurate bank details; we are not responsible for delay or mis-transfer caused by incorrect details you supply.
- **Coupons** — coupons are not refundable in cash. If a refund affects a payment that included a coupon, we refund only the cash component you actually paid.

We will issue a **GST credit note** for any refund where GST was originally charged, in line with §34 of the CGST Act.

## 10. Chargebacks

If you raise a chargeback through your bank or card network instead of using this policy:

- Your access to the affected programme may be suspended pending resolution;
- We will respond to the chargeback with documentary evidence of the original transaction and any service delivered;
- Frivolous or fraudulent chargebacks may result in account termination and recovery of associated fees.

We strongly encourage you to contact {{ACCOUNTS_EMAIL}} **before** initiating a chargeback. Most issues can be resolved more quickly through this policy than through a bank dispute.

## 11. Disputes

If you are not satisfied with the outcome of a refund request, escalate to our Grievance Officer per the [Grievance Redressal Policy](grievance-redressal-policy.md). The Grievance Officer's decision is subject to your rights under the **Consumer Protection Act, 2019** and the courts at {{JURISDICTION_CITY}}.
