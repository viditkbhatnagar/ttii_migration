## What happens next

1. We acknowledge your request within {{GRIEVANCE_ACK_HOURS}} hours and assign a **case reference**.
2. We may contact you to verify identity or clarify the request.
3. We investigate and complete the request within 30 days of receiving complete information. Where the request is complex or involves data shared with a processor, we will tell you why we need more time.
4. We respond in writing — confirming what we did, what (if anything) we could not do, and why.
5. If you are not satisfied with the outcome, you may escalate via the [Grievance Redressal Policy](grievance-redressal-policy.md), to the Data Protection Officer ({{DPO_EMAIL}}), or in due course to the **Data Protection Board of India**.

## Cases where we may decline (in part or in full)

We may, with reasons given to you in writing, decline a request where it:

- Is manifestly unfounded, vexatious, or repetitive;
- Conflicts with a legal obligation we have (e.g., to retain tax records, court orders, regulatory directions);
- Affects another person's rights or privacy disproportionately;
- Concerns data we have already lawfully erased;
- Concerns archived data we cannot access without disproportionate effort, where the data is not otherwise being actively processed.

## Fees

We do not charge a fee for the first access or correction request in any 12-month period. We may charge a reasonable fee, capped at our cost, for repeat access requests, very large extracts, or rush handling. Any fee will be quoted to you before we proceed.
