### Section D — How TTII will use the data

I have read, or had explained to me in a language I understand, the [Children's Privacy Notice](childrens-privacy-notice.md) and [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md). I understand that TTII will use my child's data:

- To deliver the programme (lessons, exams, assessments, certificates);
- To verify identity for examinations and certificate issuance;
- To communicate with me and my child about the programme;
- To comply with legal and tax obligations;
- For internal security, fraud-prevention, and safeguarding.

I understand that TTII will **not**:

- Profile or behaviourally track my child;
- Show my child targeted advertising;
- Share my child's data with marketing partners or data brokers;
- Use my child's data to train any AI model.

### Section E — Sharing with third parties

I understand that TTII shares the minimum necessary data with the following service providers to deliver the programme: **{{HOSTING_PROVIDER}}** (hosting), **{{OBJECT_STORAGE_PROVIDER}}** (storage), **{{EMAIL_PROVIDER}}** (email), **{{CONFERENCING_VENDORS}}** (live classes), **{{PAYMENT_PROCESSOR}}** (payment processing if I make a payment), and the certification partner if the programme is co-branded.

### Section F — Retention

I understand that TTII will retain my child's data for the duration of enrolment and the periods set out in the [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md), and that I may at any time withdraw consent and request deletion subject to TTII's lawful retention obligations.

### Section G — My rights

I understand I may, on behalf of my child:

1. Ask for a copy of my child's personal data;
2. Correct inaccurate or out-of-date data;
3. Withdraw this consent at any time;
4. Request deletion of my child's data;
5. Raise a grievance with TTII's Grievance Officer ({{GRIEVANCE_OFFICER_EMAIL}}) or the Data Protection Officer ({{DPO_EMAIL}}).

I understand that withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal, and may end my child's ability to continue with the programme.

1. Write to {{DPO_EMAIL}} from the email address registered above;
2. Contact the centre administrator in person and request a Withdrawal of Consent slip;
3. Send a signed letter to {{LEGAL_ENTITY_NAME}}, {{REGISTERED_ADDRESS}}.
